# Viva Voce Preparation

The assessment scheme highlights concepts, commands, tools, implementation decisions, troubleshooting, and your own repository work. Use these prompts to prepare truthful explanations based on what you actually ran.

1. **Git:** What is the difference between working tree, staging area, and commit? How did you create and merge a branch?
2. **Bash:** What does your script check? How does the disk warning threshold work?
3. **Docker:** What is an image versus a container? Explain the Dockerfile and port mapping.
4. **Compose:** Why use a Compose network? How does the application reach Redis by service name?
5. **GitHub Actions:** What triggers the workflow? What do the test and validation steps check?
6. **Jenkins:** Explain the stages in your Jenkinsfile and what causes a build to fail.
7. **Ansible:** What are inventory, playbook, task, and facts? Why is the supplied local connection safe for a demo?
8. **Terraform:** What do `init`, `plan`, `apply`, and `destroy` do? Why review a plan before applying?
9. **Kubernetes:** Explain Deployment, Pod, replicas, and Service. How did you verify readiness?
10. **Monitoring:** What does Prometheus scrape? What does the `up` query show? How does Grafana use Prometheus as a data source?

For every exercise, also prepare to explain: (a) the goal, (b) commands you personally ran, (c) the expected and actual output, (d) one error you encountered or a likely failure mode, and (e) how you checked the fix. Do not claim an error or test occurred unless it did.
